COPD Management and Spirometry - Mount Gambier

Monday, November 23

6:30pm-9:30pm

Presented By :

Dr Kerry Hancock, & Richard Parsons

Enhance your skills in identifying, assessing and managing COPD with this practical workshop designed for GPs, Nurses and Allied Health Professionals.

Explore COPD case finding and early detection, with a focus on quality-assured spirometry, accurate diagnosis and improved patient outcomes, including a practical spirometry demonstration.

LEARNING OUTCOMES:

Identify effective strategies for COPD case finding and early detection, emphasising the importance of spirometry in improving patient outcomes.

Understand the essential requirements for obtaining quality assured spirometry and learn how to interpret test results accurately.

Recognise key techniques demonstrated for obtaining accurate spirometry measurements and interpreting results effectively.

PRESENTED BY:

Dr Kerry Hancock , General Practitioner - Chandlers Hill Surgery

Richard Parsons , Respiratory Scientist, CRFS, ACP - WCH & Spirometry Learning Australia
